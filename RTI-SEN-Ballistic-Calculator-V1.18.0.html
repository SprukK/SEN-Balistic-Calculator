Advanced corrections · V1.14.0

Coriolis is exact physics (integrated over the actual RK4 trajectory) — negligible below ~300 m, grows with TOF. Spin drift uses the Litz empirical fit (1.25·(Sg+1.2)·TOF^1.83) derived from SUPERSONIC rifle bullets — unvalidated for subsonic slugs; treat as an estimate until field-verified. When ON, corrections are included in the dial above and itemised in the flags. n/a for pellets and smooth bores.
